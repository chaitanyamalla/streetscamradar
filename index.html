How it works

Local knowledge, shared before it is too late.

  1. 1
    Search where you are going

    Any city, town, postcode or street on Earth. The map travels with you.

  2. 2
    See what happened this week

    Only the last 7 days are shown, so what you read is current rather than history.

  3. 3
    Add what you saw

    Members drop a pin or type an address, and the next traveller is warned.

Reading this map well

Useful context, not fear.

Every pin is one person's account of one moment. A busy square with many reports is usually a busy square, not a dangerous one — crowds attract pickpockets everywhere on Earth. Use this to know which move to watch for, not to avoid a city.

Reports older than 7 days drop off the map on purpose. A scam that worked last spring tells you little; one that worked on Tuesday tells you a lot.

In an emergency, always contact local police first. This is a community map, not an emergency service, and nothing here is verified by authorities.
