How it works

Local knowledge, a week fresh.

  1. 1
    Search where you are going

    Any city, town, postcode or street on Earth. Drop in before you book, or while you are standing there.

  2. 2
    Read the last 7 days

    A rolling seven days, counted back from right now — not a calendar week. What you read is current rather than a decade of folklore.

  3. 3
    Add what you ran into

    Drop a pin or type an address. The next person to search that street sees it.

Reading this map well

Informed, not afraid.

Every pin is one person's account of one moment. A square with many reports is usually a busy square, not a dangerous one — crowds draw pickpockets everywhere on Earth. Read this to learn which move to watch for, not to cross a city off your list.

Reports drop off after seven days on purpose. A trick that worked last spring tells you little. One that worked on Tuesday, two streets away, tells you a great deal.

In an emergency, always contact local emergency services first. This is a community map, not an emergency service, and nothing here is verified by authorities.
